Juniors & Youth Cycling

Inspiring and developing the next generation of riders through supportive group rides, club time trials, and skills development.

Welcome Young Riders

Join Our Club Runs & Time Trials

Cambridge Cycling Club is actively committed to encouraging and nurturing junior cyclists. Whether you are looking to build group-riding skills on our social Sunday club runs or test your speed against the clock in our club time trials, there is a warm welcome waiting for you.

Sunday Club runs are typically 50 to 60 miles, split into several pace groups. Juniors generally start with the steadier groups to develop road etiquette and bunch confidence. Parents also have the convenient option to collect their young rider at the halfway café stop!

Safeguarding & Leadership

Guidance for Ride Leaders

Cambridge Cycling Club holds confirmed parental consent for all registered junior members. If an unregistered junior wishes to try a ride, they must present a completed consent form before rollout.

  • Parental Consent: Ensure a signed form is held or collected: Club Run Parental Consent (PDF).
  • Buddy System: In compliance with British Cycling Best Practice Guidelines, leaders appoint an experienced adult "buddy" to look out for the junior throughout the ride.
  • Insurance: Review our Insurance Guide for details on buddy coverage and liability.
