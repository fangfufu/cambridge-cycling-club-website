Using Routes with RideWithGPS

Click any route link to open it on RideWithGPS. Select "Send to Device" on the left of your screen and follow device instructions.

Important Tip: When navigating circular routes with café stops, disable auto-recalculation on your GPS computer. If you go off-course with recalculation enabled, your device may reroute you straight home instead of via the café!
