Membership

Membership is open to all. The membership year runs from 1st January to 31st December. New members joining on or after 1st October are given the following year inclusive. Subscriptions not renewed by 1st March will be deemed to have lapsed.

Members renewing their subscription can pay by bank transfer and need only complete a membership form if their details have changed.

The membership forms below are fillable pdf's. We prefer these to be completed using a pdf reader. Senior applications can be emailed as we accept electronic signatures. Junior applications can also be completed electronically provided they show handwritten signatures. There are more details in the form notes. The forms only become editable when downloaded. Should you not automatically be presented with this option, please download manually (Right-Click Save As).

Send completed forms to the Membership Secretary

It is important to understand membership does not include individual insurance for members. Checkout the Insurance page for more information.

Categories
1st Claim £20

If you are not currently a member of another club, join as 1st claim

2nd Claim £20

If you are a member of another club and you wish to keep it as your main club, join as 2nd claim

Day Member £5

If you want to ride a club time trial (and you are not a member of a CTT affiliated club) join as a day member at the Sign-On

Classes
Junior £5

You are a Junior if under the age of 18 in the calendar year of membership. You remain a junior until 31st December of the year in which your 18th birthday occurs

Individual £20

Applies to all applications not covered by the Junior or Household rates

Household £30

Applies to a maximum of two adults and any number of named dependants, living at the same address

Benefits
  • Potential discount at local bike shops on presentation of membership card
  • Free entry to Club time trials
  • Contribution to the expenses of the Annual Training Camp
  • Eligible to purchase Club clothing
  • Eligible for Club bursary - details here
