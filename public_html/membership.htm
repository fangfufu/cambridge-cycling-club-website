Club Membership

Membership is open to all cyclists of any ability. Find your membership class and download application forms below.

Annual Rates

Membership Classes

The membership year runs from 1st January to 31st December. New members joining on or after 1st October receive the following year inclusive.

Youth & Junior

Junior Member

£5 / year

Claim Categories

1st Claim (£20)

If you are not currently a member of another cycling club, join Cambridge CC as your primary 1st claim club.

2nd Claim (£20)

If you are already a member of another club and wish to keep it as your main club for competition, join CCC as 2nd claim.

Day Member (£5)

Riding a club time trial and not a member of a CTT affiliated club? Join as a day member at the event sign-on.

Membership Benefits

  • Free Entry to Club Time Trials: No event fee for 1st and 2nd claim members on Wednesday evenings.
  • Discounts at Local Bike Shops: Show your CCC membership card for member discounts across Cambridge.
  • Exclusive Club Kit: Order official Kalas and Nopinz custom club jerseys, bibs, and accessories.
  • Mallorca Training Camp: Club contribution to expenses for our celebrated annual spring cycling trip.
  • Club Bursaries: Eligible to apply for Club Bursaries for competitive progression.
  • Active Social Calendar: Group rides, pub gatherings, dinners, and annual trophy presentations.

How to Apply or Renew

The application forms below are fillable PDFs. Please download and complete them using a PDF reader (e.g. Adobe Acrobat). Senior applications accept electronic signatures. Send completed forms by email to our Membership Secretary.

Renewing members: You can pay directly via bank transfer and only need to complete a form if your contact or medical details have changed.

Note on Insurance

Membership of Cambridge Cycling Club does not include personal third-party liability insurance. We strongly encourage all members to maintain individual third-party cycling insurance through British Cycling or Cycling UK. Read more on our Insurance Guide.
