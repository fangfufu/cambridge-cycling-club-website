Routes

The routes below link to the website RideWithGPS.com where you can download the route. Select "Send to Device" to the left of the screen and follow the instructions.

When using RideWithGps to navigate, disable the recalculation mode for all routing options. Should you go Off Course retrace until you get the Course Found message. Should you have planned or downloaded a circular route that includes a café stop and have not disabled the recalculation mode, should you go Off Course on the way to the café, your device will create a route home rather than to the café.

The sundry route "Accident Blackspots" shows as Points Of Interest those places where members are especially cautious. Should you be aware of additional hazards please let us know.
