Privacy Policy

How Cambridge Cycling Club collects, uses, protects, and retains your personal information, and your rights under UK Data Protection legislation.

Data Controller

Who We Are

We are Cambridge Cycling Club (CCC). For the purposes of the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018, Cambridge Cycling Club acts as the 'Controller' of your personal data.

For any data protection inquiries, access requests, or corrections, contact our Membership Secretary at: membership@cambridge-cycling-club.org.uk.

When We Collect Personal Information

We collect personal data directly from you when:

  • You complete and submit a Cambridge Cycling Club Membership Application.
  • You enter or sign on for a Club Time Trial or competition.
  • You communicate with Club officials or volunteer for club activities.
Legal Basis

What Information We Collect & Why

Data Collected Purpose & Use Lawful Basis
Name, address, date of birth, telephone number Managing Club membership categories and register of members. Contract Performance
Email address, gender, cycling interests, occupation Communicating club news, newsletters, events; enabling participation in appropriate activities and volunteering; club operational support. Legitimate Interest
Next of kin & emergency telephone number Contacting nominated family or emergency contacts in the event of an accident or medical incident. Vital Interests
Photos and videos Showcasing club rides, achievements, website galleries, newsletters, and social media. Consent (withdrawable anytime)

Sharing Information

Personal data is accessible only by authorized Cambridge CC committee members. We never sell, lease, or distribute personal data to commercial third parties. Publicly shared results (on our website or local press) are limited strictly to rider names and event finishing times.

Data Retention

Membership data is retained for 1 year after cessation of membership (or deleted earlier upon written request). Time trial signing sheets are retained for 4 years (7 years in case of incident). Historical club records, trophy winners, and race results are held indefinitely in our club archives.

Security & Your Legal Rights

We maintain rigorous technical and administrative procedures to prevent unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure. Under data protection law, you have the right to:

  • Request a copy of the personal information held about you (Subject Access Request).
  • Request rectification of any incorrect or out-of-date details.
  • Request deletion of your data where no overriding legal requirement to retain it exists.
If you have concerns about our data handling, you have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): Wycliffe House, Water Lane, Wilmslow, Cheshire SK9 5AF (ico.org.uk).
This Privacy Policy is kept under regular review and was last updated on 11 October 2024.
