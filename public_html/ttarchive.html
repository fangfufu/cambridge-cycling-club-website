How to Filter Rider Records

The Rider Archive is maintained in an interactive cloud database. To view an individual rider's career results without affecting other users, create a temporary filter view using these steps:

1
Click the button below to launch the archive spreadsheet in a new browser tab.
2
Allow a few seconds for all season tabs and formulas to load.
3
Click the Filter View button located next to the blue View Only indicator.
4
Select "Create New Temporary Filter View" from the dropdown menu.
5
Click the filter arrow that appears on the right side of the Name header in cell A1.
6
In the popup menu, choose Filter by Values and click Clear to deselect all riders.
7
Search or scroll to find your desired rider name, check the box next to it, and click OK.
8
When finished reviewing times, simply close the browser tab.
