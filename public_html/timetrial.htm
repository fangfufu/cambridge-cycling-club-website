Club & Open Events

Cambridge CC organizes both Open events (advertised nationally) and Club events (for local and club riders) under the regulations of Cycling Time Trials (CTT).

Club events are held on Wednesday evenings between April and August, primarily on the classic 10-mile course at Bottisham, with additional events at Six Mile Bottom and Madingley.

Free Entry for Members: Entry is free for 1st and 2nd claim Cambridge CC members. In return, members are expected to assist with running at least one event per season as a marshal or timekeeper.

Categories

Two Competition Classes

To encourage riders of all backgrounds and setups, our club events feature separate categories.

Road Bike Category

Ideal for riders on standard road bikes. To qualify as a road bike:

  • No clip-on aero bars or tri-bars permitted.
  • No disc wheels or trispoke wheels allowed.
  • Riders must predominantly hold the drop handlebars or brake hoods.
  • Skinsuits and aero helmets ARE permitted.

Time Trial (TT) Category

Full aero setups competing for raw speed against the clock:

  • Dedicated time-trial frames and aero extensions permitted.
  • Aero disc rear wheels and deep-section front wheels allowed.
  • Aero helmet, visor, and skinsuit permitted.
  • Held in full accordance with CTT equipment regulations.

Important Rules & Marshal Guidance

Note for Marshals

Modern traffic conditions make it essential that a marshal should do no more than indicate the precise spot at which a rider should turn or the direction they should take. The responsibility for safely negotiating a turn or any other change of direction rests with the rider alone.

Juniors (Under 18)

Riders under 18 wishing to participate must present a completed CTT Parental Consent Form (Club Events Type B) in respect of each event.
