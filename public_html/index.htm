What We Do

Cycling for Every Ability & Ambition

From sociable weekend touring to competitive regional and national racing, discover how you can get involved.

Sunday Club Runs

Held every Sunday starting at Addenbrooke's. 6 groups ranging from gentle Touring (22 km/h) to Sporting+ (30+ km/h) with a halfway café stop.

Club Run Details →

Time Trials & Racing

Competitive events every Wednesday evening from April to August at Bottisham, Madingley, and Six Mile Bottom. Both road bikes and TT rigs.

Routes & Cafés

CAMBRIDGE CYCLING CLUB

Founded as Cambridge Town and County Cycling Club in 1880, we are one of the region's oldest and most active cycling clubs. Affiliated with British Cycling, Cycling Time Trials, and ECCA.
