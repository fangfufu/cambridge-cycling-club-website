Club & Rider Insurance

Clear guidance on British Cycling club insurance cover, individual liability responsibilities, and third-party protection on club rides.

The Club's Coverage

British Cycling

Cambridge Cycling Club is affiliated with and insured through British Cycling for organising and managing its regular club activities, including Sunday club runs and approved events.

  • Club Officers & Officials: Insured when acting under the Club's specific instructions.
  • Professional Indemnity: Covered under British Cycling's Professional Indemnity Insurance.
  • Event Management: Insured to organise and run club time trials on behalf of Cycling Time Trials (CTT).
Important Limitation

Club Liability Insurance does not cover claims made against individual members.

Individual Responsibility

Rider Action

Everyone participating in a group ride or club run is responsible for their own safety and actions, regardless of whether a rider is nominated as the leader for the day.

  • Club Runs: Not a formally led or coached activity; leaders are not professional coaches, and routes are not formally risk-assessed.
  • Time Trials: CTT and the Club are insured to organise events, but competitors are not covered for personal liability.
  • Bikes & Equipment: Club insurance provides no cover if your bicycle or equipment is stolen or accidentally damaged.
Strong Recommendation

We strongly urge all riders to maintain third-party liability insurance via British Cycling or Cycling UK.

Safeguarding • Best Practice

"Buddies" Supervising Juniors on Club Rides

When an adult member acts as an appointed "buddy" for a junior rider on a Sunday club run, what does British Cycling insurance cover?

What is Covered

If the "buddy" is a British Cycling member, their insurance provides third-party cover for riding or wheeling a bicycle and for negligence in respect of the handling of their own bike (e.g. if the buddy swerved and collided with the junior).

What is Not Covered

Insurance does not cover taking general legal responsibility for someone else. For example, if a buddy failed to notice that a junior was dropped from the group and came to harm, the buddy is not indemnified.
