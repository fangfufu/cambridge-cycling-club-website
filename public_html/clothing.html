How to Order Kalas Kit

We do not hold general kit stock for immediate purchase. Group orders are placed with Kalas periodically once we reach the manufacturer's minimum batch quantity. Once acknowledged by Kalas, lead time is approximately five weeks.

1
Choose your items from the 2026 Product List. Check separate sections for men and women.
2
Check sizes using the Kalas Sizing Guide or browse the Kalas Online Brochure.
3
Copy and paste the relevant product lines into an email to the Club Clothier, noting your required size and colour.
4
You will receive an acknowledgment with payment instructions. The Clothier will notify you once kit arrives for collection.

Important Order Notes:

  • Correctly made kit that does not fit: The Clothier will endeavour to sell the item to another member on your behalf.
  • Manufacturing or quality defects: Resolved directly with Kalas.
  • Racing regulation: Black kit is unofficial — British Cycling regulations require the Official White kit when representing the Club in road races.
