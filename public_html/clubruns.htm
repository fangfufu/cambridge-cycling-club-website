Newcomers Welcome

Try a Ride Before Joining

Newcomers are very welcome to join us for a couple of guest rides before deciding whether to join the Club. If you would like to try riding with us, please contact our Club Runs Captain so we can introduce you to the group leader best suited to your pace and distance goals.

Rides are predominantly on tarmac country lanes and best suited for road bikes or touring bikes with drop handlebars. Hybrid bikes fitted with relatively narrow tyres (28–35 mm) are often used in the Touring groups.

We usually cover between 60 km and 120 km (40–75 miles), with a midway café stop to socialise, refuel, and chat. Riders are always welcome to peel off early if needed.

Group Structure

6 Sunday Pace Groups

Groups are graded by average rolling speed across the ride. If you are new to road club riding, we recommend starting with a slower group to learn group etiquette before moving up.

Touring
22–24 km/h
13.5–15 mph. Steady, sociable, great for new members and endurance building.
GT
23–25 km/h
14.5–15.5 mph. Relaxed pace with slightly longer distances.
ST
24–27 km/h
15–17 mph. Moderate group pace over undulating countryside.
Intermediate
26–28 km/h
16–17.5 mph. Brisk, structured group riding and smooth through-and-off.
Sporting
27–30 km/h
17–18.5 mph. Fast tempo training ride for fitness and race preparation.
Sporting+
30+ km/h
19+ mph. High speed chain-gang pace for experienced racers and TT riders.

Horses & Winter Mudguards

Horses: Horses are common on Cambridgeshire roads, particularly near Newmarket. Give wide berth, slow down, call out early, and follow British Cycling's Horse Safety Advice.

Winter Mudguards: Over the winter months (typically Nov–March), riders are requested to fit full-length mudguards with flaps as a courtesy to those behind. Read British Cycling's Winter Guide.

Juniors: Riders under 18 are welcome but must contact us in advance. See our Juniors page for parental consent info.
