About Us

If you are looking to find others to cycle with, from the recreational to the competitive, Cambridge CC has a place for you. As one of the larger clubs in the region, we are a community of cyclists who do everything from gently paced social rides to regional and national level racing.

At the heart of the Club are the Sunday café rides. These club runs are a great way to meet a diverse and friendly crowd and explore the local lanes and cafés in a safe and welcoming environment. We usually have 4 – 6 groups each week riding at a range of different speeds and typically covering 60 – 120 km (40 – 80 miles). You are always welcome to come and try a few rides before joining. See our Club Runs page for more information and to get in touch.

The more competitive-minded cyclists in the Club take part in our Time Trials and Road Racing. The Club’s Time Trials, which are run weekly over the summer months, are an opportunity to challenge yourself as well as compete against others.

In addition to our local rides, groups often get together for informal day trips to destinations further afield, exploring regions such as the coasts of Norfolk & Suffolk or the hills of The Peak District.

The Club sponsors an annual trip to Mallorca, giving us a chance to ride together on some of the Island’s famous climbs (and even bump into a pro-cyclist or two!).

Occasional social events range from pub meet-ups to garden parties or dinners in colleges, where members try to recognise each other when not dressed in lycra.

The Club is affiliated to British Cycling and is run by a committee of volunteers who meet regularly. Meetings are open for all members to attend.
