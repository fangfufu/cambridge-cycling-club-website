About Cambridge CC

From gentle Sunday café rides to competitive regional racing, Cambridge CC has been bringing cyclists together since 1880.

Who We Are

A Home for Cyclists of All Ambitions

If you are looking to find others to cycle with, from recreational riding to competitive racing, Cambridge CC has a place for you. As one of the largest and longest-established clubs in the East of England, we bring together riders who do everything from leisurely paced social rides to regional and national level time trials and road races.

At the heart of the Club are our Sunday café rides. These club runs are a great way to meet a diverse and friendly crowd while exploring the beautiful, quiet lanes and cafés of Cambridgeshire, Essex, and Hertfordshire.

We typically have 4 to 6 groups riding every week at a range of speeds covering 60 to 120 km (40–75 miles). You are always welcome to come and try a few rides before deciding to join.

Adventures & Travel

Farther Afield & Annual Mallorca Camp

In addition to our weekly local rides, groups often organise informal day trips exploring regions further afield, such as the rolling hills of the Peak District or coastal routes along Norfolk and Suffolk.

One of the highlights of the club calendar is the annual spring trip to Mallorca. Supported by the Club, members enjoy riding together on the island's iconic climbs—such as Sa Calobra and the Coll de Sóller—and soak in world-class cycling in the Mediterranean sun (and even bump into a pro cyclist or two!).

Our social events range from casual pub meet-ups and summer garden parties to annual formal dinners held in Cambridge colleges, where members enjoy catching up when not dressed in lycra.

Organization

Run by Volunteers, Affiliated to British Cycling

Cambridge CC is a non-profit club run entirely by a committee of dedicated volunteers who meet regularly. Committee meetings are open for all members to attend, and we welcome ideas and energy from every member.
