Autumn & Winter Racing

Eastern Cyclo-Cross League

Cambridge Cycling Club has an enthusiastic contingent of cyclo-cross riders competing regularly in the Eastern Cyclo-Cross League (Eastern Cross) throughout the autumn and winter months.

Cyclo-cross is one of the most accessible, thrilling, and spectator-friendly forms of bike racing. Courses run over grass, mud, sand, wooded trails, and hurdles, packed into punchy 40- to 60-minute races. It delivers incredible high-intensity winter fitness and sharpen bike handling skills without traffic.

Local Exploration

Gravel & Trail Riding in Cambridgeshire

Beyond league racing, Cambridge and surrounding counties offer scenic bridleways, droves, and byways perfect for gravel bikes and mountain bikes. Popular local off-road corridors include:

Roman Road & Fleam Dyke

Ancient chalk tracks offering rolling, fast-draining gravel riding towards Linton and Balsham.

Wimpole & Mare Way

South-west bridleways linking through historic National Trust parkland with panoramic views.

Thetford Forest & Brecks

Purpose-built red and blue singletrack trails, wide fire roads, and sandy loops within easy driving distance.
