Autumn & Winter Racing

Eastern Cyclo-Cross League

Cambridge Cycling Club has an enthusiastic contingent of cyclo-cross riders competing regularly in the Eastern Cyclo-Cross League (Eastern Cross) throughout the autumn and winter months.

Cyclo-cross is one of the most accessible, thrilling, and spectator-friendly forms of bike racing. Courses run over grass, mud, sand, wooded trails, and hurdles, packed into punchy 40- to 60-minute races. It delivers incredible high-intensity winter fitness and sharpen bike handling skills without traffic.
